Projects

This page provides a curated directory of step-by-step methodological walkthroughs, open-source project guides, and analytical workflows for studying World History through concrete deliverables. Rather than following abstract academic exercises, learners can select an artifact to construct—such as an interactive spatial map, a digital primary-source archive, or an oral history collection—and apply historical methods through hands-on practice.
