# Other Curricula

These alternative curricula, independent study roadmaps, and community-maintained guides offer different pedagogical frameworks, thematic sequences, and reading methodologies to help self-directed learners benchmark and customize their study of World History.

- [The AskHistorians Master Book List & Reading Guide (r/AskHistorians Community)](https://www.reddit.com/r/AskHistorians/wiki/books/)
- [World History Project: Origins and 1200 to the Present (OER Project)](https://www.oerproject.com/World-History)
- [How to Learn Western History on Your Own: A 7-Step Roadmap (Knowledge Lust / Sam Rinko)](https://knowledgelust.com/how-to-learn-western-history-on-your-own-a-7-step-roadmap/)
- [World History Sequence (Saylor Academy)](https://learn.saylor.org/)
- [The Well-Educated Mind: Chronological History Framework (Susan Wise Bauer / The Well-Trained Mind)](https://welltrainedmind.com/)
- [AP World History: Modern Course Framework (The College Board)](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-world-history)
