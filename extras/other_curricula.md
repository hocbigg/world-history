# Alternative Curricula, Self-Study Roadmaps & Degree Benchmarks

This directory provides independent learners with alternative pedagogical frameworks, specialized interdisciplinary pathways, and official academic degree standards in World History. Use these resources to compare different historiographical schools of thought, explore computational methods, or audit your independent study against accredited undergraduate benchmarks.

- [Open-Access & Modular Curricula](#open-access--modular-curricula) - Comprehensive, self-paced open-course sequences and credit-aligned study paths.
- [Alternative Paradigms & Interdisciplinary Paths](#alternative-paradigms--interdisciplinary-paths) - Computational history, deep-time transdisciplinary models, and quantitative cliodynamics.
- [Official Degree Standards & Benchmark Frameworks](#official-degree-standards--benchmark-frameworks) - Institutional competency matrices and national higher education standards for history graduates.

## Open-Access & Modular Curricula

| Curriculum / Roadmap | Creator / Community | Focus & Distinctive Philosophy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [World History Project (WHP)](https://www.oerproject.com/World-History) | OER Project / Gates Ventures | An inquiry-driven, open-access curriculum featuring two complete tracks (*Origins to the Present* and *1750 to the Present*) built on scale-switching, claim testing, and historical causation. |
| [Saylor Academy World History Sequence](https://www.saylor.org/) | Saylor Academy (HIST101 & HIST102) | A fully open, self-paced undergraduate two-semester sequence mapped directly to collegiate credit-by-exam standards, utilizing curated peer-reviewed readings and cumulative exams. |
| [Modern States: History Pathways](https://modernstates.org/) | Modern States Education Alliance | Free, structured modular courses designed to achieve undergraduate credit-by-exam (CLEP), covering Western Civilization and Global History with high-yield video lectures and readings. |
| [Khan Academy: World History](https://www.khanacademy.org/humanities/world-history) | Khan Academy / College Board | A mastery-based modular progression emphasizing chronological milestones, thematic primary document analysis, and historical thinking exercises from prehistory to the contemporary era. |

## Alternative Paradigms & Interdisciplinary Paths

| Curriculum / Roadmap | Creator / Community | Focus & Distinctive Philosophy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The Programming Historian](https://programminghistorian.org/) | Programming Historian Editorial Board | A peer-reviewed, open-access curriculum providing step-by-step modular lessons in digital humanities, computational text mining, GIS mapping, and network analysis for historical research. |
| [Big History Project](https://www.oerproject.com/Big-History) | OER Project / Macquarie University | A macro-historical, transdisciplinary roadmap spanning 13.8 billion years that integrates astrophysics, evolutionary biology, and anthropology into the narrative of human history. |
| [Seshat Databank Research Framework](https://seshatdatabank.info/) | Seshat: Global History Databank | An empirical, quantitative roadmap to historical analysis applying cliodynamics, evolutionary social science, and structured cross-cultural data coding to test theories of societal collapse and growth. |

## Official Degree Standards & Benchmark Frameworks

| Curriculum / Roadmap | Creator / Community | Focus & Distinctive Philosophy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AHA History Tuning Project & Core Competencies](https://www.historians.org/) | American Historical Association (AHA) | The official United States academic benchmark establishing what undergraduate history majors should know, value, and be able to demonstrate (sourcing, historical empathy, and narrative construction). |
| [QAA Subject Benchmark Statement: History](https://www.qaa.ac.uk/) | Quality Assurance Agency for Higher Education (UK) | The official United Kingdom quality framework defining explicit graduate degree outcomes, intellectual rigor, source criticism standards, and historiographical capabilities for history degrees. |
| [AP World History: Modern Framework](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-world-history) | College Board | A standardized, unit-by-unit curricular framework from 1200 CE to the present day, organizing world history around six key themes and three primary historical reasoning skills (comparison, causation, continuity/change). |
