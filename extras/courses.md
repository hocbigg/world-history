# Great Courses: World History

This curated guide gathers high-signal university lecture series, massive open online courses (MOOCs), and comprehensive narrative audio series in World History. These resources provide structured, university-level instruction across major historical eras, regional civilizations, and global thematic transformations without requiring institutional enrollment.
